Operational Pipeline

How PhishGuard AI Analyzes Threats

A multi-stage defensive pipeline extracting lexical, structural, and syntactic markers from user-submitted URL strings without requesting external servers.

1. URL Normalization

Sanitizes input strings, standardizes URI schemes, and decomposes URLs into protocol, host, registered domain, path, and parameters.

2. Lexical Extraction

Computes 16 numerical features including dot frequency, hyphenation, subdomain count, IP hosts, brand spoofing, and risky TLDs.

3. TF-IDF Character n-Grams

Generates sub-token character n-grams (3 to 5 characters) to expose obfuscated keywords, typosquatting, and deceptive patterns.

4. Risk & Explainability

Classifies URLs into Safe, Suspicious, or High Risk, generates 0-100 risk score, surfaces threat findings, and generates PDF audits.

Local Inspection Safeguards
Defensive Rule
SECURITY_DIRECTIVE: Target URLs must NEVER be opened, pinged, or requested over network sockets.

Many automated security projects dangerously ping or crawl malicious URLs, inadvertently triggering malware payloads, DNS telemetry pings to threat actors, or IP exposure.

PhishGuard AI executes strictly in-memory lexical and syntactic token parsing, ensuring that visiting administrators and student evaluators are never exposed to live zero-day exploits.
